# WhereAmIGoing

Lunar Seers in the Bazaar Between Time only hint at their destination ("You dream of serenity.").
This mod shows the actual stage name.

- **Interaction prompt:** `Dream of Sky Meadow (2 Lunar)`
- **Ping chat line:** `Player has found: Lunar Seer (Sky Meadow) (2 Lunar).`

## Notes

- Client-side only. It works when the host doesn't have the mod; only players who have it see the stage names.
- No gameplay changes: it doesn't alter or choose where a Seer leads.
- Stage names follow the game's language, but the inserted word "of" is always English.
